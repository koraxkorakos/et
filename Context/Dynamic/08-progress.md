# debt.md

## Purpose of the Document

In this the AI keeps track of ongoing activities such that it can continue after an interruption
and prepare a commit message which can be used by the human reader upon acknowledgement and commit 
by the human developer.

Completed action (not yet committed) should be mentioned briefly and ongoing and not yet started 
concisely, but with enough information that the AI can continue after an interrupt or crash in a 
new session. 

It is continuously updated by the AI agents. Its entries should be concise and well organized.
